    开放时间： 2025.12.27 9:00 - 2025.12.29 8:59

    题量： 41 题 · 41 分

    1. 慢性肾衰竭时健存肾单位的血流动力学改变是:

      单选题，1分

      A. 肾单位血流量减少
      B. 肾小球内流体静压降低
      C. 肾小球滤过增强
      D. 肾小管血液灌注不足
      E. 出球小动脉收缩

      查看答案

      答案：C

    2. 慢性肾衰竭患者出现多尿的主要机制不包括:

      单选题，1分

      A. 抗利尿激素分泌增多
      B. 原尿中溶质浓度增高
      C. 髓 的浓缩功能受损
      D. 健存肾单位血流增加
      E. 原尿流速加快

      查看答案

      答案：A

    3. 慢性肾衰竭患者出现高钾血症最可能的阶段是:

      单选题，1分

      A. 早期多尿期
      B. 中期夜尿期
      C. 晚期少尿期
      D. 代偿期
      E. 蛋白尿初期

      查看答案

      答案：C

    4. 肾性高血压中“钠依赖型”的主要病理生理学基础是:

      单选题，1分

      A. 肾素分泌过多
      B. 体内钠水潴留
      C. 醛固酮合成减少
      D. 血管内皮舒张因子增加
      E. 交感神经活性降低

      查看答案

      答案：B

    5. 慢性肾衰竭患者肾性贫血最主要的原因是:

      单选题，1分

      A. 铁吸收障碍
      B. 红细胞寿命缩短
      C. RAS系统激活
      D. EPO生成减少
      E. 慢性失血

      查看答案

      答案：D

    6. 慢性胃溃疡底部的小动脉常可出现何种变化:

      单选题，1分

      A. 细动脉玻璃样变性
      B. 小动脉硬化
      C. 增生性动脉内膜炎
      D. 动脉粥样硬化
      E. 动脉壁纤维素样坏死

      查看答案

      答案：C

    7. A 72-year-old man takes large quantities of nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) because of chronic degenerative arthritis of the hips and knees. Over the past 2 weeks, he has had epigastric pain with nausea and vomiting and an episode of hematemesis. On physical examination, there are no remarkable fndings. A gastric biopsy specimen is most likely to show which of the following lesions?

      单选题，1分

      A. Acute gastritis
      B. Adenocarcinoma
      C. Epithelial dysplasia
      D. Helicobacter pylori infection
      E. Hyperplastic polyp

      查看答案

      答案：A

    8. A 26-year-old man has had intermittent cramping abdominal pain and low-volume diarrhea for 3 weeks. On physical examination, he is afebrile; there is mild lower abdominal tenderness but no masses, and bowel sounds are present. A stool sample is positive for occult blood. The symptoms subside within 1 week. Six months later, the abdominal pain recurs with perianal pain. On physical examination, there is now a perirectal fstula.. Colonoscopy shows many areas of mucosal edema and ulceration and some areas that appear normal. Microscopic examination of a biopsy specimen from an ulcerated area shows a patchy acute and chronic inflammatory infitrate, crypt abscesses, and noncaseating granulomas. Which of the following underlying disease processes best explains these fndings?

      单选题，1分

      A. Amebiasis
      B. Crohn disease
      C. Sarcoidosis
      D. Shigellosis
      E. Ulcerative colitis

      查看答案

      答案：B

    9. 消化性溃疡底部不含:

      D. 3个月
      E. 6个月

    11. 革囊胃是指:

      单选题，1分

      B. 弥漫浸润性胃癌

    12. 关于假小叶下列哪一项是错误的:

      单选题，1分

      A. 增生的胶原纤维分隔包绕肝小叶
      B. 假小叶内中央静脉缺如
      C. 假小叶内中央静脉位于中央

    13. 女,35岁,4年前开始经常腹泻、同时伴腹痛,吃辣或者生冷食物可诱发。肠镜见降结肠有充血水肿与多处溃疡。可能的诊断是:

      单选题，1分

      A. 克罗恩病
      B. 溃疡性结肠炎
      C. 急性肠炎
      D. 菌群失调性肠炎
      E. 家族性腺瘤性息肉病

      查看答案

      答案：B

    14. A 17-year-old girl living in the Congo has had a chronic febrile illness for 2 years. In the past 2 days she notes her urine is smoky brown. On physical examination her blood pressure is \(145/95\) mm Hg. Laboratory studies show her serum creatinine is \(3.7~mg/dL\), and urea nitrogen is \(35~mg/dL\) Urinalysis shows 4+ blood with 1+ protein, but no glucose, ketones, or leukocytes. The serum haptoglobin is decreased and Coombs test is negative. Her Hgb is \(8.5~g/dL\) A peripheral blood smear shows rare ring stage trophozoites. Immunofluorescence microscopy performed on renal biopsy shows granular deposition of IgG and C3 in glomerular capillary basement membranes. Electron microscopy shows electron-dense subepithelial "humps." Which of the following renal diseases is she most likely to have?

      单选题，1分

      A. Focal segmental glomerulosclerosis
      B. Hereditary nephritis
      C. IgA nephropathy
      D. Lupus nephritis
      E. Membranous nephropathy
      F. Acute proliferative glomerulonephritis

      查看答案

      答案：F

    15. A 7-year-old boy has become less active over the past 10 days. On physical examination, the boy has facial puffness. Urinalysis shows no blood, glucose, or ketones, and microscopic examination shows no casts or crystals. The serum creatinine level is normal. A 24-hour urine collection yields 3.8 g of protein. He improves after corticosteroid therapy. He has two more episodes of proteinuria over the next 4 years, both of which respond to corticosteroid therapy. What is the most likely mechanism causing his disease?

      单选题，1分

      A. Cytokine-mediated visceral epithelial cell injury
      B. Cytotoxic T cell-mediated tubular epithelial cell injury
      C. IgA-mediated mesangial cell injury
      D. Immune complex-mediated glomerular injury
      E. Verocytotoxin-induced endothelial cell injury

      查看答案

      答案：A
