1. Entry to a certain University is determined by a national test. The scores on this test are normally distributed with a mean of 400 and a standard deviation of 80. Which of the following is right?  
*单选题，5分*  
**A.** Tom scored better than 82.5% of the students who took the test and he will be admitted to this University.  
**B.** Tom scored less than 600 and he will not be admitted to this University.  
**C.** Tom scored better than 19.76% of the students who took the test and he will not be admitted to this University.  
**D.** Tom scored better than 75% of the students who took the test and he will be admitted to this University.  
正确答案：A
