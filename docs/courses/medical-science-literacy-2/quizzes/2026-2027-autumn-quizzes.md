    #### 一、集中趋势（共 21 分） {.exam-section .exam-section--analysis data-toc-label="一、集中趋势"}

    Here's where you have to really think about how you can best give a representative age (or ages) for the swimming class. Please see the following table:

    <div data-print-table="frequency" markdown="1">

    | Age | 1 | 2 | 3 | 31 | 32 | 33 |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | Frequency | 3 | 4 | 2 | 2 | 4 | 3 |

    </div>

    1. (3 + 3 points) Why do you think the mean and median both failed for this data? Why are they misleading?

    2. (3 + 3 points) If you had to pick one age to represent this class, what would it be? Why?

    3. (3 + 3 + 3 points) What if you could pick two ages instead? Which two ages would you pick, and why?

    #### 二、众数（共 25 分） {.exam-section .exam-section--analysis data-toc-label="二、众数"}

    Find the mode for the following sets of data. (5 points × 3)

    <div data-print-table="frequency" markdown="1">

    | Values | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | Frequency | 4 | 6 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 | 1 |

    </div>

    <div data-print-table="frequency" markdown="1">

    | Category | Blue | Red | Green | Pink | Yellow |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | Frequency | 4 | 5 | 8 | 1 | 3 |

    </div>

    <div data-print-table="frequency" markdown="1">

    | Values | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | Frequency | 2 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 |

    </div>

    1. (5 points) When do you think the mode is most useful?

    2. (5 points) When is the mode least useful?

    #### 三、数据变换（共 18 分） {.exam-section .exam-section--analysis data-toc-label="三、数据变换"}

    Suppose a new drug A can help prolong the survival time of 20 days for every colorectal cancer patient. Drug B increases survival time by 10%. The mean survival time is 100 days, the median is 60 days, and the mode is 90 days.

    1. (3 points × 3) What happens to the mean, median, and mode if every patient takes drug A?

    2. (3 points × 3) What happens to the mean, median, and mode if every patient takes drug B instead?

    #### 四、百分位数（共 5 分） {.exam-section .exam-section--analysis data-toc-label="四、百分位数"}

    A teacher records the following test scores (out of 100) for her class of 20 students:

    **Scores:** 45, 52, 71, 67, 61, 74, 76, 78, 79, 82, 83, 85, 86, 87, 88, 89, 91, 92, 94, 98

    (5 points) Calculate the 25th percentile (first quartile Q1).

    #### 五、样本标准差（共 7 分） {.exam-section .exam-section--analysis data-toc-label="五、样本标准差"}

    A sample of 5 individuals with the following weights (in kg):

    **Weights:** 45, 52, 61, 67, 75

    (7 points) Calculate the sample standard deviation.

    #### 六、选择题（共 24 分） {.exam-section .exam-section--choice data-toc-label="六、选择题"}

    *选一个最合适的答案，共 12 个作答问项，每问 2 分。*

    1. Measures of central tendency are ( )

        **A.** inferential statistics that identify the best single value for representing a set of data.  
        **B.** Inferential statistics that identify the spread of the scores in a data set.  
        **C.** Descriptive statistics that identify the best single value for representing a set of data.  
        **D.** Descriptive statistics that identify the spread of the scores in a data set.  

    2. Given the following data set, what is the value of the median? [2, 3, 5, 6, 2, 1, 3, 2, 5, 7] ( )

        **A.** 2  
        **B.** 3  
        **C.** 3.5  
        **D.** 10  

    3. Which of the following provides a measure of central location for the random variable of blood type? ( )

        **A.** standard deviation  
        **B.** mode  
        **C.** variance  
        **D.** mean  

    4. Which of the following is NOT a characteristic of the mean? ( )

        **A.** It is best used with categorical data.  
        **B.** It is unique for a specific dataset.  
        **C.** The sum of the deviations about the mean is 0.  
        **D.** It is affected by extreme scores.  

    5. The median is ( )

        **A.** The best representation for every set of data.  
        **B.** The value that divides a dataset into two equal halves when the data is arranged in ascending or descending order.  
        **C.** The most frequently occurring score.  
        **D.** The statistical or arithmetic average.  

    6. Which of the following is true regarding factors affecting variability? ( )

        **A.** The independent variable should not affect variability.  
        **B.** Random sampling results in distributions with large amounts of variability.  
        **C.** Greater variability is found in smaller samples.  
        **D.** An insensitive dependent measure will produce a lot of variability.  

    7. Use the following data set to answer the next three questions.

        | Value | Frequency |
        | --- | --- |
        | 9 | 8 |
        | 8 | 12 |
        | 6 | 2 |
        | 5 | 4 |
        | 4 | 3 |
        | 3 | 7 |
        | 2 | 3 |
        | 1 | 4 |

        1. What is the value of the mean? ( )

            **A.** 4.75  
            **B.** 6.34  
            **C.** 5.65  
            **D.** 7  

        2. What is the median? ( )

            **A.** 4.75  
            **B.** 6.34  
            **C.** 6  
            **D.** 7  

        3. How would you describe this distribution? ( )

            **A.** approximately normal  
            **B.** positively skewed  
            **C.** negatively skewed  
            **D.** rectangular  

    8. The median is a measure of ( )

        **A.** relative dispersion  
        **B.** absolute dispersion  
        **C.** central location  
        **D.** relative location  

    9. The difference between the largest and the smallest data values is the ( )

        **A.** variance  
        **B.** interquartile range  
        **C.** range  
        **D.** coefficient of variation  

    10. Which of the following provides a measure of central location for categorical data? ( )

        **A.** standard deviation  
        **B.** mean  
        **C.** mode  
        **D.** median

    #### 一、选择题（共 80 分） {.exam-section .exam-section--choice data-toc-label="一、选择题"}

    *选最合适的选项，共 8 题，每题 10 分。*

    1. 关于率的标准化，下列说法正确的是：

        **A.** 标准化后的率可以用于比较不同人群的实际死亡水平  
        **B.** 标准化率是实际测量的率  
        **C.** 标准化率的大小与标准人口选择无关  
        **D.** 直接标准化需已知各年龄组率及标准人口数  

    2. 下列哪项是“构成比”的正确特征？

        **A.** 反映某事件发生的强度  
        **B.** 各部分之和为1或100%  
        **C.** 分子是分母的一部分，且分子与分母单位不同  
        **D.** 反映某现象发生的速度  

    3. 关于“率（rate）”的说法错误的是：

        **A.** 率反映事件发生快慢或强度  
        **B.** 率的分子属于分母的一部分  
        **C.** 率有明确的时间范围  
        **D.** 率的分子和分母的单位可能不同  

    4. 要比较甲、乙两个地区人群的死亡率，但两地的年龄构成不同，应使用：

        **A.** 粗死亡率  
        **B.** 年龄别死亡率  
        **C.** 标化率  
        **D.** 构成比  

    5. A certain disease affects 2% of a population. A diagnostic test for the disease has a sensitivity of 90% and a specificity of 95%. What is the probability that a patient who tests positive actually has the disease?

        **A.** 0.182  
        **B.** 0.268  
        **C.** 0.481  
        **D.** 0.763  

    6. In a study, 30% of patients with a particular symptom have condition X. The test for condition X has a false-positive rate of 10% and a false-negative rate of 5%. If a patient with the symptom tests positive, what is the probability that they truly have condition X?

        **A.** 0.702  
        **B.** 0.775  
        **C.** 0.803  
        **D.** 0.924  

    7. In two countries, Country A has 20 deaths per 100,000 people from heart disease, while Country B has 15 deaths per 100,000. If Country A's population is 5 million and Country B's is 3 million, how many heart disease deaths are expected in each country?

        **A.** 1,000 in A, 450 in B  
        **B.** 1,200 in A, 600 in B  
        **C.** 1,000 in A, 600 in B  
        **D.** 1,200 in A, 450 in B  

    8. A standard population has 40% aged 0-14, 55% aged 15-64, and 5% aged 65+. A study population has 30% aged 0-14, 60% aged 15-64, and 10% aged 65+. If the age-specific mortality rates in the study population are 2, 4, and 15 per 1,000 for the three age groups, what is the age-standardized mortality rate using the standard population?

        **A.** 5.4 per 1,000  
        **B.** 6.0 per 1,000  
        **C.** 6.6 per 1,000  
        **D.** 3.75 per 1,000  

    #### 二、问答题（共 20 分） {.exam-section .exam-section--analysis data-toc-label="二、问答题"}

    1. 某公司推出了一款名为“SlimBiotic”的益生菌产品，宣称“临床证明有效减肥”。他们提供的证据如下：

        “我们招募了 50 名自愿者（BMI > 25），每天服用 SlimBiotic，连续 8 周。同时，我们找了另外 50 名体重相近的人作为‘自然对照组’，他们不服用任何产品。8 周后，服用组平均体重下降 2.3 kg，自然对照组平均下降 0.5 kg。两组差异具有统计学意义（p < 0.05）。因此，SlimBiotic 能有效帮助减肥。”

        1. （12 分）这个研究设计存在哪些主要问题？请至少指出 3 处。

        2. （8 分）“自然对照组”的选择可能引入什么偏倚？
